`CONTEST At the World Championship Punkin Chunkin contest in Bridgeville, Delaware, pumpkins are launched hundreds of yards. The path of a pumpkin can be modeled by h = -4.9t² + 11.7t + 42, where h is the height and t is the number of seconds after launch.`

`Use the Quadratic Formula to solve 0 = -4.9t² + 11.7t + 42.`

The coefficients are `a = -4.9`, `b = 11.7`, and `c = 42`.

The printed solution sequence is:

1. `t = (-b ± √(b² - 4ac))/(2a)`

5. `t = (11.7 + √960.09)/9.8` or `t = (11.7 - √960.09)/9.8`

Printed result:

`The approximate solutions are 4.4 seconds and -2.0 seconds.`

## `.037` — Example 2, page 44

Printed title: `Example 2 Real Roots, c Is Negative`

Printed prompt:

`Solve x² + 4x - 17 = 0 by using the Quadratic Formula.`

The coefficients are `a = 1`, `b = 4`, and `c = -17`.

The printed solution sequence is:

1. `x = (-b ± √(b² - 4ac))/(2a)`
2. `x = (-4 ± √((4)² - 4(1)(-17)))/(2(1))`
3. `x = (-4 ± √84)/2`
4. `x = (-4 ± √4 · √21)/2`
5. `x = (-4 ± 2√21)/2`
6. `x = -2 ± √21`

The final result is exact. Do not replace it with a decimal approximation.
