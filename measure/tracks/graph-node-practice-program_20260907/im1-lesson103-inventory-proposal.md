### Mixed Exercises

34. Find `UW` when `W` is between `U` and `V`, `UV = 16.8 cm`, and `VW = 7.9 cm`. Response: `8.9 cm`.
35. Find `x` if `RS = 24 cm`. Figure 17 shows `RT = 6x – 4` and `TS = 10 cm`. Response: `x = 3`.
36. Find `LO` if `M` is between `L` and `O`, `LM = 7x – 9`, `MO = 14 in.`, and `LO = 10x – 7`. Observed response: `LO = 33 in.` Calculation stage: `x = 4`.

39. `PRECISION` asks for a true statement if `P` is between `A` and `M`. Response: `AP + PM = AM`, with equivalent segment-addition notation accepted.
40. A `20 km` trail has five stops, including one at each end, with the other three evenly spaced. Response: `5 km` between successive stops. Five stops create four intervals.
41. `RACE` gives `YA = 6000 ft`, total `YZ = 3.1 miles`, and `B` as the midpoint of `AZ`. Figure 19 shows order `Y-A-B-Z`. Response: `AB = 5184 ft`, after converting `3.1 miles` to `16,368 ft`.
42. `FIELD TRIP` gives `53 miles`, `41 miles`, and total expression `3x + 16`. Response: `x = 26`.
43. `DISTANCE` gives `AM = 3x + 2`, `MJ = 3x + 4`, and `AJ = 9x – 3`. Figure 21 shows order `A-M-J`. Responses: `x = 3`; `MJ = 13 miles`.
44. A `48`-story building has `29` stories already traveled. Response: `19 stories`.
45. `CAFE` gives the learner-to-Denzel distance `r + 12`, Denzel-to-front distance `2r + 4`, and says Denzel is the exact midpoint. Response: `40 feet` from the learner to the front. The intermediate value is `r = 8`.
46. For segment `AC`, write and solve an equation to find `AB`. Figure 23 shows `AC = 3.7 cm` and `BC = 1.5 cm`. Required responses: an equation equivalent to `AB + 1.5 = 3.7`; `AB = 2.2 cm`.
47. `K` is between `J` and `L`; `JK = x² – 4x`, `KL = 3x – 2`, and `JL = 28`. Responses: `JK = 12`; `KL = 16`. The algebra gives roots `6` and `–5`; reject `–5` because it makes `KL` negative.
48. Determine whether the betweenness inequality statement is sometimes, always, or never true, and justify. Response: `always`, because `CD = CM + MD` and each proper part has positive length. Keep classification and justification as separate observed parts.
49. `C` lies between `B` and `D`; `BC = 5x + 7`, `CD = 3y + 4`, `BD = 38`, and `BD = 2x + 8y`. Responses: `x = 3`; `y = 4`.
50. Explain how to find `AC` from `AB` and `BC`, and how to find `BC` from `AB` and `AC`, when `B` is between `A` and `C`. Responses: add for `AC = AB + BC`; subtract for `BC = AC – AB`. Require both explanations.
51. Sketch `AC`, place `B` between `A` and `C`, measure `AC` and `AB`, then write and solve an equation for `BC`. Manual response: require the drawing, two recorded measurements, a consistent segment-addition equation, and its solution. Values vary with the drawing.
