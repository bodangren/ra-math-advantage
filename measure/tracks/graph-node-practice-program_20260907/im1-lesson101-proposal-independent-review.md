# G51 Lesson 10-1 proposal independent review

## Verdict

Reject the six-capability proposal until it removes one duplicate capability and corrects two evidence boundaries.

## Required corrections

1. Remove `Map a game rule and a play to an axiom and a conclusion` as a new atom. Problem 19 observes this exact role mapping. The proposal already assigns that response to the approved axiomatic-system role skill. A context-specific game version is duplicate mastery. Keep the approved-skill reuse for the comparison and mapping. Use the supported-conclusion atom only for the separately stated decision and explanation about whether the play follows or violates the rule.
2. Do not award Problem 21 evidence for testing all three diagrams unless the activity collects those three tests. The printed prompt asks for one diagram that does not satisfy all axioms and a justification. The candidate-model atom can score the selected diagram, the decision, and the cited failed axiom. Tests of the other two diagrams are unobserved intermediate work.
3. Broaden atom 6 to `Evaluate whether stated conclusions follow from axioms and diagnose an invalid inference`. Problem 22 gives three conclusions and asks whether the conclusions are correct. Score each of the three judgments separately. The first two claims follow from the stated counts. The claim that the player can collect all 40 keys does not follow because each level ends at the third key. The current atom title covers only the invalid third claim.

These changes produce five new draft atoms. If the implementation must keep six, it needs another independently observed response. The worksheet does not support a sixth atom from the current inventory.
