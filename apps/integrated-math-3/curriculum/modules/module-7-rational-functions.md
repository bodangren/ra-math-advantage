# Module 7: Rational Functions and Equations

## Overview

Students analyze rational expressions and functions, emphasizing asymptotic behavior and restrictions.

## Lessons

### 7-1 Multiplying and Dividing Rational Expressions
Simplification and domain restrictions.

### 7-2 Adding and Subtracting Rational Expressions
Common denominators and structure.

### 7-3 Graphing Reciprocal Functions
Parent function and transformations.

### 7-4 Graphing Rational Functions
Asymptotes, holes, and end behavior.

### 7-5 Variation
Direct, inverse, and joint variation models.

### 7-6 Solving Rational Equations and Inequalities
Clearing denominators and checking solutions.

## Skills Developed

- Multiply and divide rational expressions
- Add and subtract rational expressions
- Graph and analyze reciprocal functions
- Graph and analyze rational functions
- Recognize and solve direct variation equations
- Recognize and solve joint variation equations
- Recognize and solve inverse variation equations
- Solve rational equations and inequalities algebraically and by graphing
